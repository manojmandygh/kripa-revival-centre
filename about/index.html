About Kripa Revival Centre

A home away from home, since 2008.

Expert care and the warmth of an extended family, brought together to help people reclaim their future.

Our purpose

Re-Designing the future.

Kripa Revival Centre is a residential de-addiction and rehabilitation centre in Hebbal, Bengaluru. It helps people relinquish dependency on alcohol or drugs and return to an independent life.

The model combines the 12 Steps of Alcoholics Anonymous and Narcotics Anonymous with medical care, psychosocial rehabilitation, family involvement and community living.

Vision

A home away from home.

To offer residents and their families solace, security, love and kindness, together with expert rehabilitation and medical care.

Mission

Re-Designing the future.

To renew hope, rekindle life and help residents return to the social mainstream and the people they love.

Motto

Towards Excellence.

To keep learning, improving and giving each resident the most thoughtful care possible.

Founder

“Every person is rational, constructive, positive, trustworthy and full of potential.”

Benedict Reddy

Benedict founded Kripa in 2008 and continues to guide its belief that every person holds an innate capacity to correct harmful patterns when awareness, support and honest work come together.

Achievements & recognition

Trusted well beyond Hebbal.

  • 18 years of treating people - established in 2008, with more than 3000 patients treated.
  • ISO certified and licensed by the Government of Karnataka, with KPME registration.
  • Registered Mental Health Establishment, listed with the Mental Health Review Board.
  • International recognition - National Drug Agency of Maldives and Drug Court - Maldives. Clients treated from Australia, Africa, Europe, Malaysia, Maldives, UAE, U.K. and the U.S.A.
  • UTC and NIMHANS trained professionals - care guided by professionals trained in the Universal Treatment Curriculum and at NIMHANS.
  • 52% recovery rate, compared with the 8-10% recovery rate in World Health Organisation statistics.
