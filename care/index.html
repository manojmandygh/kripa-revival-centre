Therapies and services

Care for the body, mind and relationships.

A whole-person approach that joins medical supervision with counselling, education, community and family support.

Information is power

Understanding addiction changes how a person can respond to it.

Kripa combines medically informed treatment with the practical, emotional and relational work of recovery. Every service is connected to the same goal: sustained, independent life beyond treatment.

01

Medical detoxification

The beginning of treatment, supervised by a psychiatrist, physician, nurse, counsellors and care staff.

02

Individual counselling

Intensive one-to-one sessions with a designated counsellor, focused on insight, motivation and progress.

03

Group therapy

Shared recovery helps residents build trust, accountability and systems of mutual support.

04

Relapse prevention

Residents learn to recognise triggers and carry practical coping skills into life after Kripa.

05

Family intervention

Families learn to understand addiction, improve communication and prepare for reintegration.

06

Daily wellbeing

Yoga, meditation, exercise and indoor activities rebuild rhythm, physical health and discipline.

The 12 Steps

One day at a time.

Our recovery programme follows the world-renowned 12 Steps of Alcoholics Anonymous and Narcotics Anonymous, a proven and structured path out of active addiction. Guided by counsellors and the fellowship of peers, residents work through the Steps to take honest stock of their lives, repair relationships and rebuild the daily habits that keep recovery steady. Its foundation is simple: honesty, open-mindedness and willingness. And because recovery is lifelong, we connect every resident to the AA and NA fellowships for support long after they leave Kripa.
