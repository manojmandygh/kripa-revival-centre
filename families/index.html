For families

You need support too.

Addiction affects every relationship around the person. Families are a fundamental part of recovery at Kripa.

Recovery happens in relationship

Move from fear and conflict towards clarity.

Kripa helps families understand addiction as an illness without excusing harmful behaviour. Sessions address co-dependency, communication, conflict, coping skills and the practical work of reintegration.

If the person is not yet willing to stop, the family can still begin. A structured intervention can create an honest opportunity to accept treatment.

01

Talk confidentially

Explain what has been happening and ask the questions you may not know how to frame.

02

Understand the options

The team will help you assess whether residential treatment or a guided intervention is appropriate.

03

Take part in recovery

Family education and counselling build the conditions for healthier life after treatment.

Kripa may be a good fit when

  • A residential stay of around four months is possible
  • The person is open to an abstinence-based, 12-Step approach
  • Medical detoxification may be needed
  • The family is willing to participate
  • A structured daily routine is acceptable

A shorter route may not be enough when

  • Only a few days of detox are being considered
  • There is no plan for follow-through
  • The person cannot step away for residential care
  • Family involvement is not yet possible

Call us. We will be honest about whether Kripa is the right next step.

Questions families often ask

Clear answers at a difficult time.

How is the family involved?+

Family educative and counselling sessions help relatives understand addiction, improve communication and prepare for reintegration.

What if my loved one is not ready to stop?+

The Kripa team can guide the family through a compassionate, structured intervention and discuss the most appropriate next step.

How long is the programme?+

The residential programme is structured around four months. Care and session plans are adapted to each resident.

Is detox enough?+

No. Detoxification is the beginning. It is followed by counselling, psychoeducation, 12-Step work, relapse prevention and transition planning.

Can Kripa receive international residents?+

Yes. Kripa has an Ayush visa licence and has cared for residents from India and other countries.
