Two types of treatment

Alcohol de-addiction. Drug de-addiction.

Every road into addiction is different. Treatment at Kripa runs on two dedicated tracks, each with its own medical care, counselling and recovery plan.

Alcohol de-addiction

For when drinking stops feeling like a choice. Supervised detox, counselling and the 12 Steps, at a human pace.

Drug de-addiction

For dependence on drugs, prescribed or otherwise. Medical care, therapy and a structured way back to your own life.

When a family needs a way forward

Addiction can narrow life. Recovery can open it again.

Kripa treats addiction as a whole-person illness. We care for the body through medical support, the mind through counselling and education, and the relationships around each person through family involvement and community living.

We are honest about the commitment this takes. Meaningful recovery needs more than a few days of detox.
