Our place

A calm setting for focused recovery.

Two residential units in Hebbal, designed around cleanliness, community, quiet reflection and a disciplined daily rhythm.

A residential home, not an institution

Comfort and structure belong together.

Kripa offers well-lit rooms, shared and private spaces, attached bathrooms, indoor activities, an in-house kitchen, a multi-gym and a rooftop garden. Shared living in early recovery helps residents build connection and mutual support.
