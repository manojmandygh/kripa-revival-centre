Your information matters

Privacy policy.

A clear explanation of what we collect, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Privacy at a glance

Effective and last updated 21 August 2026.

Scope Information we collect How we use it When we share it Your choices Privacy contact

Kripa Revival Centre respects the private and often sensitive nature of an enquiry about addiction treatment. We use information only for clear care, communication, safety, operational and legal purposes.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Kripa Revival Centre (“Kripa”, “we”, “our” or “us”) is a residential de-addiction and rehabilitation centre in Hebbal, Bengaluru. This policy applies to information received through this website and through enquiries made by telephone, email or WhatsApp.

Clinical, admission and resident records may also be covered by separate consent forms, admission documents and care-related notices. If another notice conflicts with this website policy for a specific service, the more specific notice will apply to that service.

2. Information we may collect

We collect only information that is relevant to an enquiry, our services or the safe operation of the website and centre. Depending on how you contact us, this may include:

  • Your name, telephone number, email address, location and preferred way to be contacted.
  • Information you voluntarily share about alcohol or drug use, physical or mental health, medication, treatment history, family circumstances or support needs.
  • Messages, call details, appointment or visit arrangements and our responses to your enquiry.
  • Information supplied by a family member or another person seeking help for someone else.
  • Basic technical and security information created when the website is accessed, such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time and hosting logs.

Please use care when sending sensitive information. WhatsApp and ordinary email are convenient but may not be appropriate for detailed medical records. Share only what is needed for the first conversation unless our team asks for more information and explains the appropriate method.

Information about another person

If you give us information about a family member or another individual, you should have a lawful reason to do so and, where appropriate, their permission. We may need to verify details directly with that person before taking further steps.

3. How and why we use information

We may use information to:

  • Respond confidentially to an enquiry and explain available care, admission or family-support options.
  • Arrange calls, assessments, visits, admissions, treatment coordination and follow-up conversations.
  • Assess whether Kripa may be an appropriate and safe setting, subject to a clinical or admission assessment.
  • Protect residents, visitors, staff, systems and the centre, and respond to emergencies or safeguarding concerns.
  • Operate, secure and improve the website and our enquiry channels.
  • Meet record-keeping, regulatory, legal and professional obligations, or establish and defend legal rights.

We process information with your consent where consent is required, when you voluntarily provide information for a stated purpose, when processing is necessary to provide a requested service, or where another lawful basis or legal obligation applies. You may withdraw consent for future processing by contacting us, although this will not affect processing already lawfully completed.

4. When information may be shared

Kripa does not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information reasonably necessary with:

  • Authorised Kripa staff, clinicians, counsellors and service personnel who need it for an enquiry, assessment, care or centre operation.
  • Vetted service providers supporting hosting, email, communications, document storage, security or other essential operations, subject to appropriate obligations.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, courts or other authorities when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect a person or legal right.
  • A person you authorise us to communicate with, such as a family member, doctor or other care provider.

Third-party services

The website links to or uses third-party services including WhatsApp and Google Maps. Email, hosting and these third-party platforms may process information under their own privacy terms, potentially in other countries. Kripa does not control their independent practices. You can choose to call us directly if you prefer not to use WhatsApp or the embedded map.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No website, messaging platform or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for applicable clinical, operational, safety, accounting, regulatory or legal requirements. Retention periods vary according to the type of record and whether an enquiry leads to assessment, admission or treatment. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it.

Children and young people

This website is intended for adults seeking information about Kripa’s services. If information about a child or young person is provided, we will handle it with additional care and may require consent or involvement from a parent, guardian or other authorised person, subject to applicable law and safeguarding needs.

6. Website technology and cookies

Kripa does not currently use advertising or marketing trackers on this website. Our hosting provider may create essential security and access logs. The embedded Google Map and linked third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with them. You can manage third-party cookies through your browser or use the direct call and address details instead of the map.

7. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law and relevant care or record-keeping obligations, you may ask us to explain the personal information we hold about you, correct inaccurate or incomplete information, withdraw consent for future processing, or request deletion where retention is no longer required. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You may also raise a concern about how your information has been handled. We will acknowledge and review privacy concerns within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on this page with its updated date. Material changes may also be communicated through another appropriate channel.

9. Privacy and grievance contact

For a privacy request, correction or grievance, contact the Centre Administrator. Please do not include detailed medical information in your first email.
